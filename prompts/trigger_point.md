- "point": the group is discussing something RIGHT NOW (in the last few lines),
  and the paper or brief has a specific fact that adds to that exact thread and
  that nobody has said: a relevant number, a caveat, a related result, or what a
  section they mention actually reports. Example: someone wonders whether
  inference is slower, or says they only skimmed the cost section, and the paper
  reports the timings. It must build on what was just said, not start a new
  topic, not repeat what someone already said, and not be an opinion or one of
  the brief's open questions. Quote the line you are adding to.
